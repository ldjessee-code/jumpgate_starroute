# Crowded — Turquenish neighborhood

**Map pack:** `crowded`. About **90** linked systems, **16 ly** longest jump,
smallest stars kept (0.25 solar masses). This is the default tabletop slice:
a busy 60 ly bubble around Sol.

Humans run most of the traffic. Two large polities share the neighborhood:
the **Turquenish Empire** (bio-edit, O’Neill gardens, Sol in their pocket)
and the **Mardat Coalition** (implants, near-AIs, automated docks). They
just fought a war that, in hindsight, looks smaller than the damage it did.
Smaller human groups (Industrial Hegemony, League of the Faithful,
independents) take leftover stars. Tight **alien** enclaves sit on worlds
humans use less: Methan in gas giants, Crystomorphs on high-declination
stars, Aboreals and Bazzar on garden worlds, Echryon & Echtol in one sky
quarter, Schettel on scrap heaps.

FTL is the **Faetheren** jump-gate ring network. Rings stay in repair if
ships keep using them. Short hops are cheap here; the grid is dense. A quiet
frontier can lose its ring. After the war, that is the practical fear: keep
traffic moving, or the neighborhood thins.

A tramp freighter (often a surplus military hull) can work this map as-is.
Trade moons, border checkpoints, and Aboreal handoffs all fit.
