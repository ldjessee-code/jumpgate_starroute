# Crowded — the war

Fought over **how to improve a person**.

Turquenish culture expects gene-work. Mardat prefers implants and NAIs. For
a long time the old border was strict: checks, licensed trade, no spas for
theirs, no integrators for ours.

Then new gates opened on **frontiers** and tied distant Turquenish systems
to distant Mardat ones. Enforcement went corrupt or went missing. Mardat
citizens booked Turquenish spas. Turquenish citizens booked Mardat
integrators. Each side called it memetic warfare. Traffic shifted off the
old trunk routes and onto the messy new ones.

The war wrecked ships, yards, and people. Afterward the quarrel looks small.
Hardliners exist; they no longer have the votes. Many purity laws are gone.
Damage remains: systems that tried to **leave** (they were tired, not
subverted) got ignored. Outlying docks see few hulls. Most ships still
flying are **former military**, painted over.

Both capitals now fear the Faetheren more than each other: if traffic dies,
rings die, and the web tears. Low-population worlds hire anyone who will
haul art and news *out* and tools *in*. That is the job board for a mixed
crew on this crowded map — plus Bazzar side deals and Schettel pursuit if
you get greedy.
