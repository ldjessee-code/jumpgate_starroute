# Lonely humans — expansion

Humans found the gate net late (the tabletop notes: last ~1,500 years) and
spread faster than anyone else. On this pack you see that drive with the
neighbors stripped out.

**First hops.** Sol, then the other Sun-like hosts — Alpha Centauri, tau
Ceti, the obvious twins. No red-dwarf hamlets. If a star is too small, it
is not on this map.

**Wedges, not a sphere.** Turquenish took the first sky-third and called it
home. Mardat organized the next. Hegemony bought a later slice. Faithful
claimed a far scrap and preached at it. Independents filled leftovers.

**Why so few stars?** The mass cut is the story: this expansion skipped
M-dwarfs. Longer jumps (36 ly) keep the thin net in one piece. A lost
hub is a lost *region*, not a lost alley.

**What they tell children about aliens.** Crystomorphs, Methan, Faetheren —
old, elsewhere, maybe real. Some preachers say the rings prove a caretaker
race. Some engineers say the rings are ruins that happen to work. No one
on this map has a Bazzar contract or an Aboreal courier on speed-dial.
That absence is the tone: ambitious, lonely, and loud.
