# History

A short in-setting sketch. It is not Earth history and it is not a release
log (that lives in the repo’s `HISTORY.md`).

## Before the grid

Sol’s system is busy and well mapped. The first crewed jumps are short:
Alpha Centauri, then the other Sun-like neighbors. Most of the sky is still
a catalog, not a road.

## Opening the neighborhood

Once short hops are routine, the grid fills in wherever small stars and
known planets already sit. Crowded maps feel like this era: many red dwarfs,
short jumps, mixed habitats. Contact with non-human peoples is local, not
galactic — a handful of cultures in the same 60 ly bubble.

## Thinning the roads

Some polities stop hanging gates on the smallest stars. The map looks
sparser; remaining jumps run longer so the network does not fall apart.
**Lonely humans** is the extreme of that choice: keep Sun-like hosts, skip
alien claims, live with a thinner web.

Use whichever era matches the pack on the table. The stars do not move.
