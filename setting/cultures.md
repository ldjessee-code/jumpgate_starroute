# Cultures

Cultures are optional. They color stars after the jump grid exists. A system
can be “habitable by someone” under a pack’s rules without having a NASA
Goldilocks flag.

The multi-habitat packs reuse these people:

| People | Rough taste |
| --- | --- |
| **Human** | Oxygen / leftover rocky worlds around Sol |
| **Kessari** | Sulfur-band climates and hot, high-pressure worlds; often gas giants |
| **Methan** | Methane-breathers in stations in or around Jupiter-like giants |
| **Crystomorphs** | Living crystal; they prefer stars far from the celestial equator |

**Lonely humans** drops the alien lines. Everyone left is an oxygen-breathing
settler in the Sun-like mass band.

A little overlap is normal: one star can have a rocky temperate world *and*
a giant, so humans and Kessari (or Methan) both have a reason to care. The
map assigns one culture per system; GMs can treat the leftovers as mixed
ports.

Rename, retune, or ignore all of this. The JSON under `config/` is the rule
file; these pages are the story labels.
