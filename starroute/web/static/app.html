Jumpgate Starroute

A 60 ly map around Sol. Pick a pre-generated pack, or change jump settings in the side panel.

Later step. Load NASA’s lists, pick which star is “home,” and decide how big a neighborhood to keep. v2.0 generates maps from the shipped star list instead.

NASA star and planet lists

Use a matching pair of files from the NASA Exoplanet Archive. You can accept the ones this computer already has, or upload your own.

Cultures

        
Name each people. Species are placed first, then leftover stars go to human nations. Start from a built-in template (Methan gas-giant stations, living crystal, a sky quarter, the rim…) or a blank custom culture. You can have up to 16.
