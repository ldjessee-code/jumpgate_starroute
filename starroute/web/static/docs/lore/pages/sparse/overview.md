# Sparse — thin Turquenish grid

**Map pack:** `sparse`. About **98** linked systems, **28 ly** longest jump,
smallest red dwarfs dropped (min mass ~0.37). Same Turquenish–Mardat war.
Same peoples. **Fewer rings.**

After the shooting, traffic fell. The Faetheren do not maintain quiet
hardware. On this variant the little red-dwarf gates went dark first. What
is left is a longer-legged web between bigger stars. You still meet Methan,
crystal, Aboreals, Bazzar, Echryon/Echtol, and Schettel — just not on every
other hop. Enclaves feel like islands.

That makes the post-war fear sharper. Lose one hub and a whole chain of
frontiers goes silent. Governors pay extra for a ship that will actually
*arrive*. Border inspections are the same bio-versus-chrome nonsense, but
there is more empty sky to hide in — and more empty sky to die in.

Use this pack when you want the tabletop factions with a frontier-opera
pace: days between docks, radio lag, one Schettel hull on the scope and no
neighbors to call.
