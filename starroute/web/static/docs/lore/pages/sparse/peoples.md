# Sparse — who is still here

Read the [crowded human](../crowded/humans.md) and
[crowded alien](../crowded/aliens.md) pages for the full briefs. This page
is only what *changes* when the grid thins.

## Humans

Turquenish cylinders and Mardat automated bases are farther apart. A
colony that misses two scheduled ships feels the isolation immediately.
Hardliners have more room to hide on quiet rocks. Independents (“Other
Human”) look like a larger share of the map because there is less alien
infill and fewer tiny Turquenish red-dwarf hamlets.

The Hegemony likes this map: fewer garden worlds, more industrial hosts.
The League of the Faithful likes the distance from spas *and* integrators.

## Aliens

- **Methan** still own the giants they own. You just encounter them less
  often, and when you do there is no handy human station one short hop away.
- **Crystomorphs** remain on the high-declination sky. Their long-time
  thinking fits a thin grid; they do not mind the quiet.
- **Aboreals / Bazzar** still pair. Garden worlds are rarer on this mass
  cut, so their treaty ports are precious and well watched.
- **Echryon & Echtol** keep a quarter of the sky, with fewer linking gates
  into Turquenish space.
- **Schettel** thrive on neglect. Quiet rings and abandoned yards are their
  weather. Expect them at the far end of a long hop, not in the next system
  over.

Faetheren traffic is lumpy: busy hubs get extra care; the long empty
stretches get one ring and a prayer.
