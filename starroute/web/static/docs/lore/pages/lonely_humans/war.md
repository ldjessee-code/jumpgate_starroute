# Lonely humans — the war

Same spark as the crowded pack: **gene-work versus chrome**, frontier gates
that bypassed the old border, spas and integrators, accusations of memetic
warfare, then a real war that wrecked the fleet.

Without local aliens the war has nowhere to leak. There is no Aboreal
neutral to carry enzymes around a checkpoint, no Bazzar fixer to launder a
deal, no Schettel to strip a dead hull in the next system (humans do their
own breaking, or they don’t). Smuggling is human-on-human. So is piracy.

Aftermath on this map:

- Hardliners weaker, laws struck down, spite remaining.
- Systems that tried to secede are still called “subverted.”
- Both flags hire independents to keep Sun-like frontiers on the schedule
  so the Faetheren (or whoever keeps the rings) do not pull them.
- Mixed Turquenish–Mardat crews are a political statement, not a
  translator pool.

Play the ideological fight straight. The sky is empty enough that every
dock matters.
