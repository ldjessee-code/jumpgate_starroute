# Lonely humans — factions

Same names as the Turquenish tabletop. Different density: every star on
this map is a human argument.

## Turquenish Empire

Still the bio-edit people. Sol is theirs. O’Neill habitats, spas, engineered
crops. Without Aboreal biotech on the next dock, they are *the* genetic
superpower in this bubble and they know it. That makes Mardat more nervous,
not less.

## Mardat Coalition

Still chrome and NAIs. Without Methan giants to scare industry out of
certain orbits, they put automated plants wherever the numbers work. Their
inspectors hunt Turquenish gene-cargo at every remaining checkpoint because
there is no third party to dilute the culture war.

## Industrial Hegemony

Eleven systems in the galaxy-wide notes; a handful here. Board of directors,
not a court. They sat as close to neutral as profit allowed, then sold
hulls to both sides. After the war they buy quiet Sun-like hosts the great
powers are too tired to garrison.

## League of the Faithful

Three systems in the notes; one or two on this map if the paint is stingy.
They reject spas *and* integrators. In a neighborhood with no alien
theology to compete with, they recruit from the traumatized and the
unmodified.

## Other Human / neutrals

Colonies that tried to leave during the war, corporates, and pairs who
wanted distance from both flags. On the lonely map they are not squeezed
between alien enclaves — they are the actual frontier. Their fear is the
same: if Turquenish and Mardat only serve “strategic” stars, these docks go
dark and the ring follows.

Crews are still mixed. The galley argument is purely human: edit the child,
or socket the adult, or do neither and call it holiness.
