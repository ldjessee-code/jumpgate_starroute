# Lonely humans

**Map pack:** `lonely_humans`. About **58** linked systems, **36 ly**
jumps, stars in the **Sun-like** band (about 0.66–1.5 solar masses). **No
local aliens** on the map. No Methan giants claiming the neighborhood, no
crystal skies, no Aboreal gardens, no Bazzar docks, no Schettel yards.

This is not “humans are alone in the universe.” It is “this 60 ly bubble
has no one else in it.” The Faetheren rings are still here — someone built
them — but the builders do not live on these docks. Rumors of older races
are history-class, not traffic control.

What *is* here is human politics at full volume: Turquenish bio-edit
culture, Mardat chrome, a corporate Hegemony, a tiny Faithful theocracy,
and a scatter of independents. They fought the same ideological war. They
still need the gates used. They just have no alien neighbors to trade with,
hide behind, or blame.
