# Crowded — Turquenish neighborhood

**Map pack:** `crowded`. About **90** linked systems, **16 ly** longest jump,
smallest stars kept (0.25 solar masses). This is the default tabletop slice:
a busy 60 ly bubble around Sol.

Earth-led humans run most of the traffic. Two big polities — the
**Turquenish Empire** (bio-edit, O’Neill gardens, Sol in their wedge) and the
**Mardat Coalition** (chrome, NAIs, automated docks) — just finished a war
that now looks petty. Smaller human blocs (Industrial Hegemony, League of
the Faithful, independents) fill the leftover sky. Tight-knit **alien**
enclaves sit on the worlds humans do not want: Methan in gas giants,
Crystomorphs on high-declination stars, Aboreals and Bazzar on garden
worlds, Echryon/Echtol in one sky quarter, Schettel on scrap heaps.

FTL is **Faetheren jump-gate rings**, maintained by usage. Short hops are
cheap here; the grid is dense. If a frontier stops seeing ships, the
Faetheren neglect that ring. That is the post-war panic: keep the web
humming or lose the neighborhood.

A Scarab-class tramp (often a surplus frigate) can work this map without
changing the catalog. Kalvor-style trade moons, border checkpoints, and
Aboreal handoffs all fit.
