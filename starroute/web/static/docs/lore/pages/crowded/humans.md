# Crowded — human polities

Both great powers are **mostly mainstream humans** (about 60–70%). Other
peoples live among them; they do not run the Empire or the Coalition.

## Turquenish Empire

Open to **biological and genetic editing**. Spas, progeny work, engineered
crops, bio-recyclers. They built the big rotating habitats — a couple of
dozen O’Neill colonies in the wider setting; on this local map, Sol and the
first sky-wedge are theirs. Charismatic administrators, failing bioreactors,
and hardliners who still talk about genetic purity are all in play.

They claimed on the order of **39 systems** before the war (galaxy-wide
notes). This pack is the **local pocket around Sol**, not the whole Empire.
Sol is always theirs, even when a Methan giant sits in the same system.

## Mardat Coalition

**Mechanical / electronic** improvement: implants, drone convoys, Near-AIs.
Smaller population, higher output. Compact habitats and asteroid bases
instead of garden cylinders. Inspectors with neural implants. Ms. Betty–style
war-surplus medics. Galaxy-wide they had about **38** member systems; here
they hold the next sky-wedge over from Turquenish space.

## Smaller human blocs

- **Industrial Hegemony** — a corporate board over a handful of systems.
  Factories first, flags second.
- **League of the Faithful** — a tiny theocracy (a few systems in the notes).
  Suspicious of both spas and integrators.
- **Other Human** — independents, corporates, and pairs that sat out the war
  or tried to leave when the shooting started. Governors still hire
  independents to haul media out and tools back in, hoping the gates stay
  lit.

On the map, leftover rocky worlds around Sol go to these human groups after
the alien enclaves take the habitats they actually want.
