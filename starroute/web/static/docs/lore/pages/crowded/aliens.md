# Crowded — local aliens

Aliens in this neighborhood are **tight-knit**. They do not sprawl the way
humans do. On a crowded map they still show up in the cracks: a Methan
giant here, a crystal sky there, a garden world the Bazzar almost razed.

## Methan

Gas-giant dwellers. Start huge and grow huger (jellyfish / airship). They
barely speak to anyone except, rumor says, Crystomorphs and Faetheren — and
that takes a long time. Industry in orbit around “their” giant can be read
as a threat. One warning (maybe). Then sterilization. Many crews just leave
when the Methan EM signature lights up. Their ships are the largest things
that fit the rings (hundreds of meters across).

## Crystomorphs

Silicon life. Talk in light. Think in long time. A mobile stage is
person-sized; the rest is not. Older than humans on the gate net. They
prefer stars **far from the celestial equator** on this map — out of the
thickest human traffic.

## Aboreals

Scent-language, nearly pacifist, unmatched biotech. Humans only ever see
the gentle version. The **Bazzar** tell a different story: they started
farming a garden world that was already occupied; the Aboreals erased them
from the system overnight. Treaty now: Bazzar handle security and trade for
Aboreal goods. Cross the line at your own risk.

## Bazzar

Hive insectoids. “Queen” is a leftover human word — three sexes, and a
promoted Queen becomes sexless. Each hive has a personality. They love a
deal. Fixers on trade moons (Kwez at a Kalvor-style dock) are how Aboreal
cargo reaches human space.

## Echryon and Echtol

Related cephalopods: one water, one land, twelve limbs, speech in shape /
color / texture. Written language started as flip-books. They mingle,
border mostly Mardat space, and have a couple of gates into Turquenish
sky. Translators recommended.

## Schettel

Scavengers. Flexible, poison-proof, digest almost anything. No factories
anyone has seen, no roads, no treaties that last. Junkyard worlds and
breaker’s fleets. Hire them to scuttle a hull. Do not expect them to stay
bought. On this small 60 ly slice they may not **own** a painted system —
they still show up as traffic and trouble.

## Faetheren

Nobody has seen one. They maintain, build, and decommission **gates**.
Usage-based: busy rings get care (and sometimes a second ring in a century);
quiet rings fade. They talk through NAIs. Docking with their ships is
forbidden — they cut free or self-destruct. Speculation from wreckage:
biology plus chrome plus wetware. They do not paint as a culture on the
map; they are the infrastructure.
