# Crowded — gates and travel

**FTL** is the Faetheren ring network. No one remembers a sky without it.
Rings are huge. Methan hulls are the largest that pass (spheres or cylinders
hundreds of meters across, up to a couple of kilometers long).

This pack’s **16 ly** jump cap is the dense-neighborhood version: short hops
between small stars as well as Sun-like hosts. In setting terms, the
Faetheren kept (or recently refreshed) a lot of little rings because this
sky was busy before the war.

In-system travel is whatever the dock can afford: chemical, ion,
reactionless. Aboreals and Methan seem to use reactionless only.

Post-war facts of life on this map:

- Independent crews matter because both navies are short of hulls.
- Border stations still scan for the *other* kind of enhancement.
- A quiet system is a system that may lose its ring.
- Schettel listen to traffic. Busy gates are also hunting grounds.

If you need a first port, pick a Mardat-side trade moon a few gates off the
tense border (the tabletop “Kalvor-3” shape) or a Turquenish cylinder whose
bioreactors are failing (the “Eden Spiral” shape). The catalog names on the
map are real stars; the station names are yours.
